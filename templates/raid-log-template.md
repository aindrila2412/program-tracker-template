**RAID** stands for **R**isks, **A**ssumptions, **I**ssues, **D**ependencies.

| Term | Meaning | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| Risk | Something that might happen and would affect the program | Has a likelihood; track mitigation |
| Assumption | Something treated as true without proof | Validate it by a date, or it becomes a risk |
| Issue | A risk that has happened, or a problem now | Needs an owner and an action |
| Dependency | Something the program needs from someone or something else | Name the provider and the date needed |

## Scoring guide

Use Low / Medium / High for likelihood and impact. A simple priority rule:

- High impact and High likelihood: **High** priority, review weekly
- One High, one lower: **Medium**, review every two weeks
- Otherwise: **Low**, review at milestones

Adjust the rule to fit your team.
